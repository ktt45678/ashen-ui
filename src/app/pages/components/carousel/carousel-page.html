Carousel

Content slider with prev/next arrow buttons and stretching dot indicators. Each child sn-carousel-item becomes one slide. A single options object (Swiper-compatible) tunes autoplay, loop, slides-per-view, spacing, direction and more, and navigation can be fully customised or placed anywhere.

Accessibility

  • The carousel host has role="region" and aria-roledescription="carousel".
  • The region is labelled via aria-label (defaults to "Carousel" via SnCarouselIntl).
  • Each slide has role="group", aria-roledescription="slide", and a "Slide n of m" aria-label.
  • Inactive slides are marked aria-hidden="true" and receive the inert attribute so their contents are unreachable by keyboard.
  • Prev/next buttons use <button type="button"> with accessible labels from SnCarouselIntl.
  • Dot indicators have per-dot aria-label ("Go to slide n") and aria-current="true" on the active dot.
  • ArrowLeft / ArrowRight on the carousel region move the active slide.
  • Autoplay pauses while keyboard focus is inside the carousel. For a visible rotation control, bind a button to the playing signal and the pause() / play() methods (WCAG 2.2.2).
  • Pointer swipe on the track supports touch, mouse and pen: the track follows the pointer live and snaps to the nearest slide on release. Use [snCarouselNoSwipe] to exempt interactive/selectable regions, or allowTouchMove: false to disable dragging entirely.
