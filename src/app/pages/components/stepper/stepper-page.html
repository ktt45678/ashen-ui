Accessibility

Follows the WAI-ARIA Breadcrumb-inspired list pattern with wizard progress semantics.

  • Steps are rendered in an <ol> (ordered list) reflecting wizard progress order.
  • Each step header is a native <button>. The active step receives aria-current="step".
  • The active step's content region has role="region" and aria-labelledby pointing to the step's header button.
  • Step markers and connector lines are aria-hidden="true" - screen readers hear only the label and state from the button.
  • Keyboard navigation: ArrowRight/ArrowLeft in horizontal mode, ArrowDown/ArrowUp in vertical mode move between steps. Home/End jump to first/last step. Focus wraps.
  • Disabled and linear-blocked steps are skipped by the focus key manager.
