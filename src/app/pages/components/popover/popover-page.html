Popover

An anchored overlay panel that toggles on trigger click. Use it for quick settings, pickers, and contextual actions that don't warrant a full dialog. The panel closes on outside click, Escape, or when the consumer calls close().

Accessibility

  • Follows the WAI-ARIA Dialog Pattern in non-modal form - the panel has role="dialog" without aria-modal.
  • The trigger element automatically receives aria-haspopup="dialog", aria-expanded (updated on open/close), and aria-controls pointing to the panel.
  • Focus moves into the first tabbable element in the panel on open (via CDK ConfigurableFocusTrapFactory).
  • Focus returns to the trigger element when the panel closes.
  • Escape closes the panel and returns focus to the trigger.
  • Clicking outside the panel (backdrop click) closes it without focus disruption.
  • Overlay cleanup is handled in ngOnDestroy - no memory leaks on navigation.
