Drawer

An edge-anchored modal panel that slides in from any side of the viewport, or covers it entirely. Use Drawer for inspectors, settings panels, and editing surfaces that stay open while the user works. The drawer traps focus, blocks body scroll, and closes on Escape, backdrop click, or the built-in close button.

Accessibility

  • Follows the WAI-ARIA Modal Dialog Pattern. The panel has role="dialog", aria-modal="true", and aria-labelledby pointing to the title element.
  • Focus moves into the first focusable element in the panel on open (via CDK ConfigurableFocusTrapFactory).
  • Focus is trapped within the drawer - Tab cycles through the panel's focusable elements and cannot reach elements behind the backdrop.
  • Focus returns to the element that had focus before the drawer opened.
  • Escape closes the drawer and restores focus - even when mask=false.
  • Clicking the backdrop closes the drawer when mask=true (CDK backdropClick() subscription).
  • Body scroll is blocked while the drawer is open (CDK BlockScrollStrategy).
  • The close button's aria-label defaults to 'Close' (overridable via closeLabel input or provideSnIntl()).
